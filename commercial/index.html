Restaurant Services

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and a system that's undersized or failing reveals itself as a sweltering cook line and complaints at the tables. Fryers, ranges, and grills dump enormous heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air straight out of the building. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and guests notice the drafts at the door and the stuffy dining room right away. We keep temperatures and airflow where your operation needs them, then leave behind written reports and service logs after each visit. That paperwork demonstrates a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and covers the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and expensive water damage from clogged condensate lines. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Every installation and inspection we perform covers heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

Each hour without climate control costs a business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. In a retail store, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. For a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Lafayette HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Lafayette and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

Our technicians are equipped to handle any commercial HVAC emergency, including failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, and control board lockouts. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring heating or cooling as quickly as possible, then put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records and facility files.

Maintenance Programs

Proactive HVAC Care for Businesses

Stopping failures before they happen is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment.
